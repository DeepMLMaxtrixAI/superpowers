# Superpowers Repository Strategy

## Role
Superpowers is a low-priority incubator / parked repository until a sharper strategic purpose is defined.

It may be used for:
- contained experiments
- small utilities
- early-stage concept exploration
- work not yet ready for the flagship or runtime-core repos

## What belongs here
- Clearly bounded experiments
- Lightweight utilities with a defined purpose
- Prototype work that is not yet part of the mainline product stack

## What does not belong here
- Flagship product logic
- Canonical runtime infrastructure
- Undefined sprawling branches without a clear objective
- Work that should already live in TrendChatGPT or runtime-core

## Consolidation decisions
1. Do not treat this repository as a peer empire to the flagship or runtime-core repos.
2. Only keep work here if it has a simple one-sentence purpose.
3. Move any durable or strategic work out of this repo once validated.
4. Freeze or archive branches that do not justify continued attention.

## SWOT sweeping-the-floor matrix
### Strengths
- Safe place for experimentation without contaminating the flagship
- Useful as a buffer for early ideas and tiny tools

### Weaknesses
- Weak visible strategic identity
- Easy place for undefined work to accumulate
- Lower compounding value than the main repos

### Opportunities
- Serve as a clean incubator for bounded ideas
- Reduce clutter in the flagship and runtime-core repos
- Provide a holding zone before promotion or archival

### Threats
- Becomes a junk drawer
- Consumes attention without producing durable assets
- Creates false parity with repos that matter more

## Sweeping-the-floor rules
- Every project here must have a one-sentence purpose.
- Promote validated work out of this repo.
- Archive stale branches aggressively.
- Do not let this repo compete with the mainline portfolio.

## Immediate next actions
- Define whether any current work deserves promotion.
- Archive low-value or stale experiments.
- Keep this repo small, clear, and explicitly secondary.

## Decision rule
If the work is strategic and durable, move it out.
If it is exploratory and bounded, it may live here temporarily.
If it has no clear purpose, freeze or archive it.
