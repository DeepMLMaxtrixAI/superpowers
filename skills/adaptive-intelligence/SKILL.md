# Adaptive Intelligence Civilization

## Overview

The Adaptive Intelligence Civilization is a multi-archetype framework that maps signal types to specialized intelligence pillars. Each archetype embodies a distinct dimension of analysis—order, innovation, economics, security, and chaos pressure—enabling holistic interpretation of complex political, economic, and social signals.

## The 5 Archetype Pillars

| Archetype | Domain | Role |
|-----------|--------|------|
| **Law_Givers** | Order | Establish rules, norms, and legal structures that govern systems |
| **Explorers** | Innovation | Drive discovery, experimentation, and frontier expansion |
| **Oracles** | Economics | Interpret economic forces, market dynamics, and resource allocation |
| **Guardians** | Security | Protect assets, enforce boundaries, and manage threats |
| **Gremlins** | Chaos Pressure | Introduce disruption, test resilience, and surface hidden failures |

## Parenti Interpretation

The Parenti Interpretation is a political-economy signal type (`Parenti_Interpretation`) routed to the `Political_Economy_Analyzer` engine. It applies the following framework:

```
Parenti Interpretation
│
├── Economic imperialism theory
│     └── Analyzes foreign policy through corporate and capital interests
│           driving imperial interventions (Oracles archetype)
│
├── Political critique of drug policy
│     └── Examines drug war policies as instruments of political control
│           and economic intervention (Law_Givers + Guardians archetypes)
│
└── Critics:
      ├── security explanations      → Guardians archetype perspective
      ├── geopolitical strategy      → Explorers archetype perspective
      └── policy complexity          → multi-archetype synthesis
```

### Archetype Mapping

| Parenti Component | Primary Archetype | Secondary Archetype |
|---|---|---|
| Economic imperialism theory | Oracles (Economics) | Explorers (Innovation) |
| Drug policy critique | Law_Givers (Order) | Guardians (Security) |
| Critics: security explanations | Guardians (Security) | — |
| Critics: geopolitical strategy | Explorers (Innovation) | Oracles (Economics) |
| Critics: policy complexity | All archetypes (synthesis) | Gremlins (Chaos Pressure) |

## Signal Routing Integration

The Parenti Interpretation is integrated into the signal-routing system:

```
Parenti_Interpretation → Political_Economy_Analyzer
```

The `Political_Economy_Analyzer` engine provides:
- **economic_imperialism_analysis** – corporate and capital interest mapping
- **drug_policy_critique** – drug war as political/economic instrument
- **critics_framework** – triangulated counter-perspective analysis
- **geopolitical_assessment** – great-power competition lens
- **policy_complexity_mapping** – multi-causal, non-reductive analysis

## How It Works

When a `Parenti_Interpretation` signal is received:

1. **Economic Layer** – Oracles archetype maps capital flows and corporate interests
2. **Policy Layer** – Law_Givers archetype evaluates drug policy structures and legal frameworks
3. **Security Layer** – Guardians archetype assesses threat narratives and security rationales
4. **Geopolitical Layer** – Explorers archetype traces strategic and regional interests
5. **Complexity Layer** – Gremlins archetype stress-tests reductive explanations

## Use Cases

- Analyzing US foreign policy through an economic imperialism lens
- Evaluating drug war policies as tools of political control
- Triangulating competing explanations (security vs. economics vs. geopolitics)
- Multi-archetype synthesis of complex policy signals

## Key Principles

- **Multi-perspective** – Always triangulate through all applicable archetypes
- **Non-reductive** – Policy complexity requires synthesis, not single-cause attribution
- **AI_SAFE** – All interpretations are bounded by evidence and structured reasoning
- **Intrinsic Value** – Every archetype cycle unlocks measurable analytical value
